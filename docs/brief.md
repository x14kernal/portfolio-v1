# Project brief: Personal Portfolio Site (v1)

**Problem it solves:** gives you one link to send anyone (a recruiter, a friend) that shows your work.

**Must-have features:**

1. A short "About me" section.
2. A "Projects" section, listing the Habit Tracker (link + one screenshot + 2 sentences).
3. A way to contact you (email, GitHub, and LinkedIn if you have one).
4. Works well on a phone-sized screen.

**Definition of Done:**

- [ ] Deployed and reachable from a free subdomain.
- [ ] Loads fast, no console errors.
- [ ] Looks correct on a small (phone) screen.
